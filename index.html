The HALT Difference

What Makes HALT the Safest Choice?

HALT Fire is the fluorine-free, PFAS-free replacement for AFFF and legacy wetting agents. As cancer is now the leading cause of death among firefighters, we are committed to being part of the solution, not the problem.

Non-Toxic Formula

Independent third-party tests confirm HALT is "Practically Non-Toxic", performing better than household dish soap. Safe for daily use.

Non-Carcinogenic

Zero PFAS chemicals. Zero CA Prop 65 components known to cause cancer, birth defects, or reproductive harm. Independently verified.

Eco Friendly

No phosphorous or PFAS chemicals that permanently contaminate waterways and harm fish and wildlife. Fully biodegradable formula.

Easy Cleanup

All natural. After extinguishment, a quick rinse with water is all you need. No hazmat, no professional cleanup, no damage to equipment.

Food & Environment Safe

NSF White Book Listed since 2016, independently certified safe for use on and around food processing equipment and food preparation surfaces.

Non-Corrosive

Non-damaging to pumps and fittings aboard fire engines and industrial equipment. Confirmed through ongoing testing by firefighting customers.

Any Surface

Acts as a superior heat barrier for welding, soldering, plumbing, and more, adheres at any angle and penetrates porous materials.

Zero Reignition

Absorbs into material surfaces, providing a continuous protective coating that will not evaporate, preventing reignition long after application.

Official Announcement, April 2026

HALT Fire Acquires GreenFire.
Launches HALT Industrial.

In April 2026, HALT Fire acquired GreenFire, the pioneer of PFAS-free industrial firefighting foam, and launched HALT Industrial, bringing patented non-toxic technology to fire departments and industrial operators nationwide. The acquisition was covered by Morningstar, Benzinga, and over 186 media outlets.

Existing GreenFire customers retain full access to all products, certifications, and SDS documents. No disruption. Same trusted technology, now under the HALT Industrial brand with expanded support and capabilities.
